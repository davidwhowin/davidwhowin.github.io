Nova Drop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Nova Drop ("the game") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: we don't ask for your name, email or any account, and we don't collect personal information ourselves. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to do that, as described below.

Information the game stores on your device

Your progress, coins, settings and high scores are saved only on your own device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID (on Android) or identifier for advertisers (on iOS, only if you allow it);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app details such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps).

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings (Privacy options).
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings (Google, then Ads). On iOS you can turn off tracking in Settings (Privacy & Security, then Tracking).
  • A paid "Remove Ads" option, where offered, turns off interstitial and banner ads.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We don't change difficulty or scores based on ads or on your device.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
